Data Deletion Instructions

For the Meta app “Lantern Lights Publisher”, used by the Lanternlight Tales Facebook Page and the @lanternlight_tales Instagram account.

What we store

Lantern Lights Publisher is a private tool used only by the channel’s owner. No one else logs in to it or connects an account to it, so it does not store personal data about other users.

The only information about other people that can pass through it is what Meta provides for comments on our own posts: the comment text, the commenter’s public name or username and an account identifier. Any copy we keep is deleted within 30 days.

How to request deletion

  1. Email kavindu18602@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include your Facebook name or Instagram username so we can find any records.
  3. We will delete any data we hold about you within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete it yourself

You can delete your own comments on our posts at any time on Facebook or Instagram. Once a comment is deleted, we can no longer access it.

Contact

Email kavindu18602@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.
