Lanternlight Tales

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to the Lanternlight Tales Facebook Page, the @lanternlight_tales Instagram account, and the Meta app “Lantern Lights Publisher”.

Who we are

Lanternlight Tales is an animated storytelling channel that publishes original short videos on Facebook and Instagram. Lantern Lights Publisher is a private tool used only by the channel’s owner to manage those two accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can log in to it.

What the app does

  • Publishes our own videos and images to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account.
  • Reads performance statistics (such as views, reach, watch time, likes and shares) for our own posts.
  • Reads, replies to and moderates comments left on our own posts.

Information we access

Statistics. Meta provides these as totals for each post. They do not identify individual people.

Comments on our posts. When someone comments on one of our posts, Meta makes the comment text, the commenter’s public name or username, an account identifier and the time of the comment available to us. We use this only to read and reply to comments, and to hide or remove spam and abuse.

We do not access anyone’s private messages, contacts, friends list, email address, location or any other profile information. We do not collect information from anywhere other than our own posts.

How we use and store it

  • Statistics are stored on our own server to track how our videos perform.
  • Comment data is processed when we read or reply to comments. Any copy we keep is deleted within 30 days.
  • Access tokens issued by Meta are stored securely on our server and are never shared.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone.
  • We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than running our own accounts.
  • We do not use Facebook Login or ask anyone other than the owner to connect an account to the app.

Third parties

Facebook and Instagram are operated by Meta Platforms, and your use of them is covered by Meta’s Privacy Policy. Our server is hosted by a cloud provider and is used only to run the tool described here.

Your choices

You can delete or edit your own comments on our posts at any time on Facebook or Instagram. To ask us to delete any information we hold about you, follow the steps on our data deletion page or email us.

Children

Our content is family-friendly, but the app is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect information from, children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
